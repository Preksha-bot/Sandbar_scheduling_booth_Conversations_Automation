## The evening before (15 minutes)

1. **Phone:** sign in to the alias in Chrome or Safari. Open Owen's and Nadia's links. Add each to the home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen, or ⋮ → Add to Home screen).
2. **Laptop:** open a **private/Incognito window** (Ctrl+Shift+N) and sign in **only** as the alias, then open the Sheet there. (A browser signed in to several Google accounts makes Step 1's window and the phone pages fail with "PERMISSION_DENIED".) Check the **Sandbar** menu appears to the right of **Help**.
   - Backup for Step 1: put the new show's files in a Google Drive folder on the alias account beforehand, and use **Sandbar** → **Step 1 (other way) · Add files from a Drive folder…** with that folder's address.
3. **Practice state:** the Sheet already holds Toronto (practice load, Steps 2–3 and Day 30 done). If it doesn't: **Sandbar** → **Practice: load the Toronto example** → OK, then **Step 2**, **Step 3**, **Day 30** (it asks to add Toronto's bookings & emails file: click **OK**).
4. **Day 90 for the demo:** add `tests/fixtures/toronto/example-invoices-MADE-UP.csv` with **Sandbar** → **Day 90 · Add the invoice list…**, then **Sandbar** → **Day 90 · Count paying clinics**. **This invoice list is made up** (no Toronto invoices were supplied). Say so when you show it: it only demonstrates the screen.
5. **Nadia's demos:** on Nadia's page → **My demos**, tap **held** on two and **no-show** on one, so day 60 has something to show.
6. **The new show's files:** when they arrive, save them in one folder on the laptop. Don't open or change them.
7. Optional: keep the Toronto state by **File** → **Make a copy** → "Sandbar – Toronto (demo)". Then you can show both.

## The run (about 15 minutes)

### 1. What it's for (30 seconds)

> Toronto cost $9,000 and Nadia couldn't say what it brought in. 46 of 80 real conversations were never written to. This is Erin's Thursday, done in a Google Sheet she already has: one row per real conversation, the right next step, a ready draft from whoever had the conversation, and Nadia's answer at day 30, 60 and 90.

### 2. Nadia's answer on Toronto (2 minutes): phone, **Results**

- **Day 30:** 7 demos, 3 trials, 24 wrote with nothing back, 46 never wrote. Every person lands in the same pile as Erin's hand recount (80 of 80).
- **Not sorted yet:** Derek and Ben, written to by Owen with no outcome tapped. "Never guessed."
- **Day 60:** the demos Nadia tapped held or no-show.
- **Day 90:** paying clinics and the "has it paid for itself" line. *Made-up invoices, to show the screen.*
- **Was it worth it:** $9,000, the cost per demo or trial, and the break-even line.

### 3. The new show, live (6 minutes): laptop, the Sheet

1. **Sandbar** → **Start a new show (clears the last one)** → **OK**. Say: "The do-not-email list stays: whoever asks off stays off, every show."
2. **Sandbar** → **Step 1 · Add the show's files…** → **Choose files** → select the badge export and the booth notes → **Add these files**. Read the summary out loud ("110 badge scans → In: Badge export…"). Say: "Only what exists two days after the show. The bookings file comes in at day 30."
3. **Sandbar** → **Step 2 · Colour personal lines yellow**. It opens the **Highlighter** tab: one row per personal part, with tab and row. Open **In: Voice notes** and show a yellow cell where only the personal sentence is red: "Erin can delete the red part. If she doesn't, it's still left out, and any draft with a personal word is stopped."
4. **Sandbar** → **Step 3 · Make the lists and drafts**. Read the message: real conversations, hot/warm/cold, drafts ready, check first, and **drive-bys and non-prospects get nothing**.
5. Open the **People** tab. Pick one person and read across: *which email and why*, *can we email?* and *why*, *priority* and *why*, *next step*, *what they said*, *how they were matched*.
6. **Sandbar** → **Step 4 · Answer the Check first questions…**. Say: "Only real decisions get here: Ottawa had 2, not 14 rows." Show one card: the whole person in one place, the green suggestion and why. Click **Ask Owen** on one, then answer the other (**Don't email**): the green line at the top shows the result, and the lists update by themselves.
7. On Owen's phone page (next section), show the question waiting at the top under **Questions for you**. Tap an answer: back in the window, click **Refresh** and the card is gone.

### 4. Owen's phone (3 minutes): phone, **My list**

1. Pull to refresh (or reopen the page): the new show's list.
2. **1. Asked for something** first: promises before anything else.
3. Open one draft (**Read the draft**): three or four lines, the specific thing they said, the booking link (never a promised date), the unsubscribe line at the bottom.
4. Tap **Open in Gmail** (on the laptop) or **Open in my email app** (on a phone): a new email opens with the To, a hidden copy to hello@, the subject and the draft. **Don't send.** Go back. ("My email app" opens whatever the device uses for email, e.g. Outlook on a Windows laptop. That's why the Gmail button is there.)
5. Tap **Done: I sent it**, then an outcome (**interested**). Each button says **Saving…**, then a green **✓ Saved** line appears under the card (it takes 2–4 seconds). "That's Owen's whole job after a show."
6. Scroll to **Waiting**: there's no send button, only a note for Erin and **remove me**, because nothing has been written to them yet.

### 5. Nadia's phone (1 minute): **My list**, **My demos**

- Her own people (anyone she scanned, and French conversations, in French).
- **My demos**: one tap after each demo.

### 6. Scoring the 15 (the brief's test)

For each of the 15 people the client picks, open their row on **People** (Ctrl+F their name):
- **Appears once:** one row, all their scans, cards and notes listed under "how they were matched".
- **Priority** and **next step**: the two columns, with the reason beside each.
- **The draft mentions something specific:** "what they said" and the draft column. Nothing personal (compare with the **Highlighter** tab).
- **Nobody who shouldn't be emailed gets a draft:** students, vendors, draw entries, "remove me" have no draft, and the reason says why.

### 7. If they gave the new show's after-show export

**Sandbar** → **Day 30 · Add the bookings & emails file…** with that file → **Day 30 · Count the results** → phone **Results**.

### 8. Close (1 minute)

- **Costs:** $0 (Google Sheets and Apps Script, no subscription, no AI). Erin's time is estimated at 3–4 hours a show; it hasn't been timed yet.
- **When it's wrong:** every row shows its reason; uncertain rows wait in "check first"; Owen reads every draft before sending.
- **What it can't fix:** conversations nobody wrote down, Owen's single export login, Nadia's full calendar.
- Honest numbers: on three test shows it had never seen, it got 37%, 75% and 52% right on the first run, and **never drafted to anyone who shouldn't be emailed**. The weak spot is the specific detail line on unfamiliar wording.

## If something goes wrong in the room

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| No **Sandbar** menu | Reload the page (F5) and wait 5 seconds. |
| "Authorization required" | **OK** → alias → **Advanced** → **Go to Sandbar follow-up (unsafe)** → **Allow**. If the popup is blocked, open Owen's phone link instead: it asks for the same permission in the page. |
| "PERMISSION_DENIED" in Step 1 or on a phone page | The browser has several Google accounts signed in. Switch to the Incognito window with only the alias, or use **Step 1 (other way)** with the Drive folder. |
| A step seems stuck | Wait for the black "Running script" box to say **Finished**. Step 3 takes about 20 seconds. |
| The phone page says "You need access" | The phone isn't signed in to the alias. Use the laptop instead. |
| Google itself is down | On the laptop: `node tools/run-show.js "<folder with the new files>"`, then open `out/<folder>/report.html`. Same engine, same drafts. |
